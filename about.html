GET TO KNOW ME

A little more
about me.

There's more to me than what's written on a resume. Here's a little glimpse of who I am, what I enjoy and where I see myself going.

01 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE PAGE

Hello, I'm Chahat.

I'm currently an undergraduate business student, figuring things out one experience at a time. I'm interested in business, how people think and work together, and the little things that make an idea come to life.

Outside academics, I'm someone who enjoys travelling, discovering new places, good food and capturing random moments through photography. I like having new experiences, meeting different people and occasionally stepping into things I haven't tried before. There's always something interesting to take away from a new place or situation.

02 / A FEW THINGS THAT MATTER TO ME

What makes me, me.

Curiosity

I tend to ask questions and look beyond what's immediately in front of me. Whether it's a new subject, an unfamiliar place or an interesting conversation, I enjoy finding out more.

Taking Initiative

I don't mind taking the first step when something interests me. Being involved in school responsibilities taught me that sometimes you learn the most by simply putting yourself out there.

Communication

I enjoy conversations, exchanging ideas and hearing different points of view. I believe good communication is as much about listening and understanding as it is about expressing yourself.

Trying New Things

I'm always up for experiences that take me somewhere different. I may not have everything figured out, but I'm happy to explore, make mistakes and learn along the way.

03 / WHAT'S NEXT?

Still figuring it out, and that's okay.

I'm at a stage where there's a lot to learn and even more to explore. I want to understand the business world beyond textbooks, build practical skills and discover the areas that genuinely interest me.

For now, I'm focused on making the most of my college experience, taking up opportunities that come my way and giving myself room to try different things. I don't have every step planned out yet, but I know I want the journey to be interesting, challenging and worth looking back on.
