GET TO KNOW ME

A little more
about me.

There's more to me than what's written on a resume. Here's a little glimpse of who I am, what I enjoy and where I see myself going.

01 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE PAGE

Hello, I'm Chahat.

I am currently pursuing my undergraduate studies in Digital Business and Entrepreneurship at IIM Bangalore. I'm someone who enjoys learning new things, meeting people and experiencing life beyond the usual routine. I like keeping myself occupied with different interests, although I'm equally fond of days when I have absolutely nothing planned.

Outside academics, you'll usually find me planning a trip, exploring a new place, trying out different food, or clicking random pictures of sunsets and little details in nature. I enjoy reading, especially a good thriller, and I'm also someone who genuinely appreciates a good night's sleep. Travelling, eating, photography, reading and simply exploring what's around me are some of the little things that make me happy.

02 / A FEW THINGS THAT MATTER TO ME

What makes me, me.

Curiosity

I enjoy asking questions and understanding the reason behind things rather than simply accepting them as they are. I like exploring unfamiliar topics, looking at situations from different perspectives and learning something new whenever I get the chance. I'm always interested in knowing a little more about the things that catch my attention.

Initiative

I believe in taking responsibility instead of always waiting for someone else to make the first move. My experiences with school responsibilities have helped me become more comfortable with taking charge, participating and making the most of opportunities that come my way. I enjoy being involved and contributing wherever I can.

Communication

I enjoy expressing my thoughts, having conversations and exchanging ideas with people. Being involved in English communication activities at school helped me understand the importance of speaking confidently, listening carefully and knowing how to put an idea across. I also enjoy hearing different opinions because there's often something new to learn from a conversation.

Growth

I don't think growth always has to come from big achievements. Sometimes it comes from trying something unfamiliar, learning from a mistake or handling a situation differently than before. I value these experiences because they gradually shape the way I think and approach things. I believe there's always room to improve, regardless of where you are.

03 / WHAT'S NEXT?

Still figuring it out, and that's okay.

I'm at a stage where there's a lot to learn and even more to explore. I want to understand the business world beyond textbooks, build practical skills and discover the areas that genuinely interest me.

For now, I'm focused on making the most of my college experience, taking up opportunities that come my way and giving myself room to try different things. I don't have every step planned out yet, but I know I want the journey to be interesting, challenging and worth looking back on.
